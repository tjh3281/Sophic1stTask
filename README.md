## 1. Assembly Automation

- Onelier:

Machines and robots that build, sort, mark, and pack products automatically →  
replacing slow, error-prone manual handling.

- Benefits  
  (i) Higher productivity : Systems operate around the clock, 24/7, without fatigue, drastically cutting cycle times and boosting output volumes.

  (ii) Consistent Quality : Precise, repeatable actions eliminate human error, ensuring every part matches exact specifications with fewer defects.

  (iii) Lower Labor Costs: Routine work shifts to machines, reducing direct staffing needs and freeing workers for higher-value programming or oversight roles

  (iv) Improved Safety: Moving hazardous or repetitive physical tasks away from human operators lowers workplace accidents.

- Solutions

  can be tracked and identified from packing through to shipment.

2. **Laser Marking Equipment** - x

- What is Laser Marking Equipment ?
  To perform 1D and 2D laser tracking codes on electronics. It is used in semiconductor
  manufacturing for product tracking and process standardization.

- Benefits

  (i) Full traceability : Permanent 1D/2D codes let you track every product through its entire
  lifecycle from production line to end customer.

  (ii) Long lasting identification : Laser-etched serial numbers, part numbers, and logos resist
  wear, heat, and handling, so markings stay readable for the product's life.

  (iii) Consistent process control : Automated marking integrates directly into your production
  line, applying the same clean, accurate code to every unit with no manual effort.

- Function

  (i) Traceability Tracking: Applies 1D and 2D codes directly onto sensitive electronic
  components.

  (ii) Process Standardization : Integrates into production lines for product handling and quality
  tracking.

  (iii) Permanent Identification**:** Etches serial numbers, part numbers, and logos that resist wear.

3. **Automated Handler Equipment** - x

- What is Automated Handler Equipment?
  A robotic system that uses robotic arms to assemble, disassemble, switch products between
  test trays, and sort items by quality

- Benefits

  (i) Faster handling & sorting : Robotic arms assemble, switch, and move products between test
  trays far quicker than manual handling → keeping your line moving and boosting
  throughput.

  (ii) Accurate quality sorting : Products are automatically sorted by quality level, so good and
  reject units are separated consistently, with no human mix-ups.

  (iii) Reduced manual labour : Repetitive tray loading and product switching are automated,
  freeing your operators for higher-value work and cutting handling errors.

- Functions

  (i) **Component handling**: Moves parts at high speed for placement and sorting.

  (ii) **Product switching**: Transfers items into different process trays for testing.

  (iii) **Quality sorting**: Separates items based on quality levels during manufacturing.

- What is Machine Vision ?
  Automated visual inspection and defect detection systems using industrial cameras, lenses,
  lighting, and high-speed software to improve manufacturing quality and efficiency.

- Benefits
- Functions

(i) Sophic AOI : Sophic AOI scans super high resolution image data to identify anomalies and defects. The system is powered by Kabowd vision software that designed for high speed and precise vision inspection application, which able to support multi-camera system in parallel.
